# V8 — Early Entry Window

- Entry may trigger in the first 35% of the current candle, or within the signal candle plus the next two candles.
- The two-candle grace window is reconstructed from recent EMA crosses or fresh candle turns and is still gated by current direction, EMA extension, ATR displacement, volatility, oscillator conflict, and risk checks.
- It does not force an entry: a stale/extended or reversed setup remains rejected.
- This is a paper-trading strategy change, not a profit guarantee. Validate in PAPER mode and review entry/exit logs.
